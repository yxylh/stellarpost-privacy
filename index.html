Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-16 · App: Stellar Post

This Privacy Policy is a practical draft for the Stellar Post app. It is not formal legal advice. Operator: individual developer of Stellar Post. Contact: stellarpost@163.com. Version 1 focuses on Sign in with Apple.

1. Who we are

Stellar Post (“we”) is operated by an individual developer. Contact: stellarpost@163.com.

2. Information we process

3. How we use information

We use the information above to operate and improve the service, complete in-app purchases, moderate unsafe content, prevent fraud, comply with law, and respond to your requests.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Cloud / infrastructure providers that host our servers and databases
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchase; Apple processes payment details)
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users and the service

Open letters are visible to other users. Private letters are available to the intended participants as needed to deliver the service.

5. Retention and security

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for legal or security reasons. After account deletion we delete or anonymize data in a reasonable time; backups may retain copies briefly. We use reasonable safeguards (such as HTTPS and access controls). No system is perfectly secure.

6. Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or account closure by emailing stellarpost@163.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience. If you are a minor, use the app with a parent or guardian’s guidance. We do not knowingly collect children’s data for marketing.

8. International users

Data may be processed on servers operated by our cloud providers. If data is transferred across borders, we take reasonable steps to protect it.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy and post the new version here or note it in the app. Continued use after changes means you acknowledge the updated Policy.

10. App Store privacy labels (summary)

Depending on final shipping features, labels may involve user content (letters), purchases, identifiers, and diagnostics. Fill App Store Connect based on what you actually ship.
